# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.
They will communicate the reason for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Maelstrom community spaces, including the
GitHub organization, Discord server, community events, voice calls, and direct
messages sent in connection with project participation. It also applies when an
individual officially represents Maelstrom in public spaces.

## Reporting a concern

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to
**<conduct@maelstrom-ai.co>**. For an incident happening now in Discord, you may
also mention the `Moderator` role or privately message a moderator. Do not post
sensitive evidence or personal information in a public channel.

Include the following details when available:

- What happened and where it happened
- When it happened
- Who was involved
- Links, message IDs, or screenshots that preserve relevant context
- Any immediate safety concern
- The outcome you want the project team to consider

You do not need to confront the person involved before reporting. A person who
receives a report must recuse themselves if they are involved in the incident
or have another conflict of interest.

The project team will review each report promptly and fairly. It will share the
report only with people who need the information to investigate, protect the
community, or meet a legal obligation. It will protect the reporter's identity
as far as practical and explain any necessary disclosure when it can safely do
so. The team will retain only the evidence needed to resolve the report, review
an appeal, identify repeated conduct, or meet a legal obligation.

Retaliation against a reporter, witness, or person who participates in an
investigation violates this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will use these guidelines when deciding consequences. They may
skip steps when the severity, impact, or repeated nature of the behavior
requires a stronger response.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other behavior that the
community considers unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning that explains the violation and how
to correct it. The project team may request a public apology when that would
repair public harm without exposing a reporter.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through one incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with conditions for continued participation. The
person may not interact with specified people, including through unsolicited
messages, for a defined period. Violating these conditions may result in a
temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation, sustained inappropriate behavior, or
failure to follow a warning.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from specified or all Maelstrom community
spaces. The person may not contact the people involved in the incident or evade
the ban during this period. Ban evasion may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or
aggression toward or disparagement of a protected class.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from Maelstrom community spaces. The project
team may also exclude the person from official representation or future
community participation.

## Appeals

Email **<conduct@maelstrom-ai.co>** to appeal an enforcement decision. State the
decision you are appealing and explain any relevant error, missing context, or
new evidence. Do not relitigate the decision in public channels or contact the
reporter.

A community leader who did not make the original decision will review the
appeal when possible. Anyone with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves.
The reviewer may uphold, reduce, increase, or reverse the consequence and will
communicate the result privately.

Discord may separately enforce its own Terms of Service and Community
Guidelines. Maelstrom cannot review or reverse actions taken by Discord.
